About Our Workshop

Manufacturing Furniture with Integrity in Vijayawada

Crafting bespoke furniture solutions for homes, hotels, and offices with solid wood frames, high-density foam, and heavy-gauge steel.

Andhra Furniture and Interiors is a dedicated furniture manufacturing unit established in Singh Nagar, Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh. We craft bespoke furniture solutions for residential homes, corporate offices, hotels, and educational institutions.

Unlike mass-market retail outlets that sell pre-packaged, fixed-size particle board imports, we construct our furniture on solid wood frames, heavy-gauge steel, certified high-density PU foams, and commercial-grade laminate finishes.

Whether you need a space-saving 3-seater sofa bed, an orthopedic mattress to relieve chronic spinal pain, modular workstation tables for an expanding office, or sturdy dual benches for a school classroom, our craftsmen build each piece to your exact required length, depth, and finish.
